Why it stays fast on small hardware

Track, don’t re-detect identity. Each face gets a tracker ID, so the heavy recognition step runs only for new or uncertain faces.

Vote across frames. An identity is confirmed only after consistent matches, which removes one-frame mistakes.

Auto-governor. The detector size shrinks or grows by itself to hold your target frame rate.

Person’s name

Guided enrollment captures 5 samples while you look straight, turn to each side, tilt up and smile. Varied angles give the most accurate matching.

Or add from photos

Use clear, front-facing photos with one person each. 3 to 6 photos per person works well.

Quality tips

Face the light, keep one person in frame while enrolling, and avoid sunglasses or masks. For 90% or better accuracy, enroll in the same lighting where the device will be used.

The database is saved in this browser. Export it to move your enrolled people to another device.
